Nocta ECG for Polar - Support

Need help? Email emregozen@yahoo.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

My Polar H10 won't connect

Wet the strap electrodes, wear the strap so the sensor wakes up, make sure Bluetooth is on and Nocta has Bluetooth permission, and close other apps that may be connected to the H10 (such as Polar Flow).

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the Pro screen in Nocta and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Nocta Pro.

Is Nocta a medical device?

No. Nocta is for general wellness and fitness only and does not diagnose or detect any condition. If you have concerns about your heart, contact a doctor.
