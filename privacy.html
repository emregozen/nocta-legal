Nocta ECG for Polar - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Nocta ("the app") is developed by Emre Gozen. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Heart data stays on your device

Recording metadata, computed heart rate and HRV metrics, and raw ECG samples are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. Nocta has no user account and no server of its own. Your recordings and health metrics are never uploaded by the app.

Bluetooth

Nocta connects to your Polar H10 over Bluetooth to stream ECG, heart rate, and RR-interval data. This connection is device-to-device and does not involve any Nocta server.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments for Nocta Pro are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. Nocta never sees your payment details. To manage subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription history, and basic device and app information (such as app version, OS version, and country/storefront). It does not receive your ECG, heart rate, or HRV data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Exports and deletion

Exporting a PDF report or CSV creates a file on your device that you control and share yourself. Deleting a recording in the app removes it and its ECG data from your device permanently. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

What we don't do

Nocta shows no ads, does not use third-party analytics or tracking, does not sell data, and does not use your health data for advertising or data mining.

Not a medical device

Nocta is for general wellness, fitness and informational purposes only. It does not diagnose, treat, or detect any condition.

Children

Nocta is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
